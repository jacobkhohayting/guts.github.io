Rules

  1. The contest has 8 rounds of 3 problems each and lasts 60 minutes. The clock is the same for every team and starts automatically at the scheduled time — nothing needs to be clicked.
  2. You can only see the round you are currently on. Submitting a round locks it permanently and immediately reveals the next round. You cannot go back to a previous round or change submitted answers.
  3. You may submit a round with blank answers to move on, but you will not be able to return to it.
  4. Answers must be exact and fully simplified unless the problem says otherwise. Plain text or LaTeX both work, for example 42, \frac{3}{7}, 2\sqrt{5}, \frac{\pi}{4}. The preview under each answer box shows exactly what will be submitted.
  5. Each correct answer earns the points shown next to the problem. There is no partial credit and no penalty for wrong answers. You will not be told which answers were right; only total scores are published, on the leaderboard.
  6. When the timer reaches zero, whatever is in your answer boxes is submitted automatically and the contest ends.
  7. Work only with your own teammates. No outside help, and no calculators or computer algebra systems. (Organizers: edit this list to match your rules.)
  8. If something looks wrong (page won't load, timer looks off), refresh the page first — your typed answers are kept on your device — then contact the organizers.
